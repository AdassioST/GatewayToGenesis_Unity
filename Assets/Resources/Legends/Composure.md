#mechanic #spellweaving 

The level of stress, sanity, and meter of the internal [[Coherence]] of any [[Spellweaver]].

To lose [[Composure]] is to lose oneself, if all [[Composure]] is lost at the level of surrender, the [[Soul Leitmotif]] fractures, enveloping the [[Spellweaver]] in pure [[Dissonance]]. 

When the cocoon hatches in a wave of [[Discordant Interference]], what walks out is an [[Atonalis]], no longer a person nor [[Spellweaver]] but a broken hungry husk permanently trapped in the torturing paradox of an existence without [[Coherence]].

Thus, [[Composure]] is the most important metric for any [[Spellweaver]], it is visible in the [[Soul Leitmotif]] as it gets cloudy with muddy black [[Dissonance]] that becomes blacker and cloudier the closer a [[Spellweaver]] is to surrender and [[Corruption]].

[[Composure]] consists of 5 stress levels on the [[Soul Leitmotif]] that mirror that of the growth phases of an [[Atonalis]] from [[Nascent]] to [[Primal Discordia]]:

- Pristine: The perfect rest state for a [[Soul Leitmotif]], it is vibrant in color, beautiful, and emits light with the color of its primary binding. Usually represents a peaceful and [[Consonance]] filled state of mind. Momentarily, a [[Soul Leitmotif]] can become pristine on intense moments of joy.

- Clouded: This is the normal state for most [[Spellweaver]]s, it has the vibrant light of the primary binding but certain parts of the inside of the gem have black [[Dissonance]] which gives it the primary color a clouded view of the interior structure, it doesn't look translucid as a Pristine state, but it doesn't have physical damage. Despite being murky, this state is usually where a [[Spellweaver]] acts with most confidence, mirroring that of [[Sectile]] [[Atonalis]] where they have rationalized enough their own wound to be fully functional but without the awareness necessary to cripple them in their own despair. [[Formless Masses]]
 
- Fractured: This is the first sign of actual damage to the [[Soul Leitmotif]], black cracks appear visible in the outer casing, not just on the inside cloudiness anymore. This state is necessary for triggering a [[Motif Awakening]] as the seeping cracks allow the [[Soul Leitmotif]] to adopt and embellish new [[Ornament]]s from the created space within. It is healthy, and almost inevitable, for a [[Spellweaver]] to reach this state but it's key to remember that any wound is merely a source of power, and what gives power can also crack you open. If the wound continues to fester it's a dangerous pivot to surrendering oneself.

- Spiraling: This is the final level before the point of no return, and it has a signature of black fractal spirals that begin to mirror the own internal chaos of the [[Spellweaver]] that is falling to the abyss of despair. The spirals on the surface are similar to [[Sky Glass]] but devoid of its beautiful tactile qualities, and this is the closest state that a [[Spellweaver]] can be to becoming an [[Atonalis]] before falling to the point of no return. **This is the last point where anyone can intervene to save them.** Any breach further on the hungering fractal spirals and the [[Soul Leitmotif]] will undergo its permanent transformation when it is fully consumed by the black of the [[Dissonance]], turning itself into a [[Dissonance Core]].

- Surrender: Once a [[Soul Leitmotif]] reaches this point, there's nothing anyone can do to save them. It has a clear indicator of two distinct crack sounds, the first is when the threshold is reached, when the [[Soul Leitmotif]] went beyond its maximum stress point, and the [[Spellweaver]] is on its last remnants of [[Coherence]] that tether their [[Composure]]. The second crack is the inevitable moment when the [[Spellweaver]] vocalizes their last words. Upon reaching this state the [[Dissonance]] cocoon emerges siphoning the user inside of it, transforming the [[Soul Leitmotif]] into the very [[Dissonance Core]] of the [[Atonalis]]. The location of the [[Dissonance Core]] is often the Wearable State of the soul gem in the last moment the dying [[Consciousness]] assigned value to anything. **The refusal to process pain feels exactly like mercy right up until the moment it permanently turns you into a predator.**

The state of the [[Soul Leitmotif]] [[Composure]] directly aligns with the internal state of mind of the [[Spellweaver]]:

A Pristine State of Mind, a Clouded State of Mind, a Fractured State of Mind, a Spiraling State of Mind, and the Surrendered State of Mind.

Despite the visual cues, these are only visible during the resting state of the [[Soul Leitmotif]]. If it's on the Wearable State, none of the Clouded or Pristine levels are visible, the only moment the [[Dissonance]] is visible on a non resting state is only until the [[Spellweaver]]'s [[Composure]] reaches Spiraling or below, and it will become visible as [[Discordant Interference]] pulsing with flickering dark alongside the color of whichever binding they're channeling.

Any real deity, like the [[Auric Aria]] or [[Outer God]]s can't become [[Atonalis]]. However, they do require [[Composure]] to function. This is particularly notable in the [[Auric Aria]] which did reach the surrender point during the [[Eighth Cycle]] and provoked the [[Lost Cycle]] for uncountable [[Cycle]]s. Her eventual return due to the pleas of [[Humanity]]gave her a sliver of [[Composure]] to move from spiraling into recovery to clouded Golden Sovereign of the [[Old Testament]].

The [[Auric Aria]] has only reached the surrendered state once, and on its very border which is why she came back to creation. Only does in the [[Alternate Hollowing]] she fully embraces the surrendered state and triggers the end of the [[Known Universe]] back into static. Typically, she hovers between spiraling at her worst, fractured on a regular, and clouded at her most vivid. Only after the sequence of the [[Singer of Paradise]], and in the apex of the [[Parlor of the Moon]] does she attain Pristine [[Composure]] after reconciliation with [[Selenea]] in the end of the [[Sonata]] of [[Arcanoria]].

### Societal Implications

Due to the [[Composure]] meter reflecting the internal state of mind of a [[Spellweaver]], there are several layers of implications on how this information is shared, externalized, or even weaponized through relationships, and even diplomacy. 

Usually the sacredness of sharing the inner view of the [[Soul Leitmotif]] is considered a moment of vulnerability, which is precisely why it's mostly equipped in the wearable state and only transformed into the resting version due to forced exhaustion, sleep, or sacred meditation. It's uncommon to have a [[Soul Leitmotif]] in a resting state without a personal guard of sorts, and it's impossible to do so without consent unless the [[Spellweaver]] physically passes out.

Even travelling parties or with allies the [[Spellweaver]] tends to keep in secret their dormant [[Soul Leitmotif]] whether in a pocket or directly by clutching it somewhere in their body during sleep. (Like hiding money under the pillow)

Furthermore, this also extends to a personal level for relationships. To deliberately invite someone to see the inside of your soul is a deeply personal and intimate gesture of courtship and romance between [[Spellweaver]]s, and in some cultures the exposure of the bare soul is indistinct from showing a naked body, which makes by proxy the dormant state as sacred as the act of physical intimacy, and to some degree both can be intertwined.

On a societal level this also reflects in diplomacy, where envoys most often than not tend to be non [[Spellweaver]]s, as [[Humanity]] without a [[Soul Leitmotif]] is useful precisely to keep undercover the internal state during the negotiations. Even if there's a [[Spellweaver]] present, most notably with any [[Head of State]], they tend to use it on the Wearable State as both class, clothing, and as means to prevent the visualization of their internal state of [[Coherence]].

However, there are some cultures that do require honesty as means to establish any formal treaty, this type of society tends to align with the [[Aureus Pillar]] or [[Waltz Pillar]]s, and they present negotiations through a pedestal where the dormant "sacred" state of a [[Soul Leitmotif]] is an anchor to the truthfulness of both parties, sometimes this technique is required in unethical practices such as torture or court trial to get information that prevents perjury due to the microscopic flashes of the [[Soul Leitmotif]] and its relationship to the [[Key of Attunement]].

This later half however does not mean a [[Spellweaver]] can't lie, on the contrary, this very basis of informational manipulation is a core tenet of [[Illusory Magic]], to bend the [[Luminance]] of a [[Soul Leitmotif]] to deliberately guide, frame, blackmail or create false evidence. The race of [[Illusory Magic]] is similar to the [[Strand]] [[Detective Magic]] applications, creating a loop of [[Spellweaving]] mastery of having measures to lie and counter measures to detect lies.

This very principle on [[Illusory Magic]] is one of the great contributions to [[The Principles of Magic]] by the partnership of [[Luminaire]] and [[Amadea]], the respective [[Mythical Virtuoso]]s of [[Luminance]] and [[Void]], the partnership gets even pushed further with [[Cordelia]] making the intersection of these [[Mythical Virtuoso]] the founding school of proper [[Illusory Magic]] and [[Detective Magic]].

The implications of having a visual cue to the [[Soul Leitmotif]] is both important religion, diplomacy, and culture wise, heavily varying on the interpretation of the [[Pillars]] of [[Civilization]]. Lying becomes much more sophisticated and hard to identify if the evidence itself can be contradictory.
