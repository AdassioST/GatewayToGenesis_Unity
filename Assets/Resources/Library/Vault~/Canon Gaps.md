# Canon gaps

What the game still shows without a vault source, and what the vault itself is missing or contradicts. Written
during the first lore import (September 2026, Age of Desolation first). Nothing here was invented: where the vault
had no words, the game's old text stays and is listed below. `ImportReport.md` (regenerated by every import) lists
the passages the import left out and every `[[link]]` that still has no note.

## Game text still waiting for canon

Kept as it was; each needs a vault note (or a line in one) before it can change.

- **Resources**: Food ("Peaches, perhaps?"; the vault's Age 0 food is the Auric peach, now linked from Food's
  tooltip), Duskstone, Elderwood, Faith, Research.
- **Sections**: Academia, Golden Hymn Citadel, Great Fields *and* The Great Fields (two sections with near-identical
  names), Heartlands, Industrial Valley, Residential District, The Outskirts, Vital Resource (banner lines and
  quotes), and Old World Remnants' quote. The Age of Desolation's note mentions "sparse lookout towers sending
  explorers to the nearby areas", which may be where The Outskirts, the Hollow Watchpost and the Scout belong.
- **Buildings and units** (13): Ancient Windmill, Crystal Mine, Decaying Hut, Field Kitchen, Grave Warden, Hollow
  Watchpost, Maiden's Hold, Moonlit Grove, Ruin Hunter Camp, Scout, Stonebound Shack, Timber Camp, Wasteland Archer.
  The vault only names Decaying Hut and Field Kitchen (as examples in Game Logic.md).
- **Technologies** (6): Agricultural Renewal, Efficient Rations, Horology, Reconstruction, Rites of Harvest,
  Woodcraft Mastery. The vault names only Reconstruction (Game Logic.md).
- **Stored food** (Resources/Food/Pantry, section Stored Food): Dried Auric Peaches, Earth-Beans, Bitter Roots,
  Deep-Rooted Grain and Ash-Bread are the vault's own crops (The Inescapable Hunger.md) and quote it; **Behemoth
  Meat** has no vault source (the vault only names the later Age of Behemoths): its description is empty. Every food
  value, spoilage rate, forage amount and land yield is a proposal. Icons are placeholders borrowed from other art
  (plant, Elderwood2, Duskstone, beef). The "Stored Food" section's banner line is game text.
- **Expedition lines** (Resources/Events/Expeditions.ink): The Ballad of the Infinite Hum (Fallen Auric Resonators,
  three verses) and The Golden Orchard's Seeds quote the vault where it has in-world lines (Crumbling Infrastructure,
  Relics as fertilizer, Resonator Arts' infinite hum, the abandoned crops); the connective prose between them (the
  camp in the tower's shadow, two towers holding one note, the third completing the chord, the rows gone wild) is
  placeholder. The vault's note that resonators were made by Aurelian and fixed by Daedalus in Ages I is a design
  note (Gateway To Genesis.md), so it is not quoted.
- **Phases** (12): every Phase line on the time bar ("A solemn hymn of penance."...). The vault lists the phases
  and their Echo, nothing more.
- **Cycle names** on the time bar (scene, TimeSystemLogic): Twilight, Guvierse, Naniurka, Edging, and "Overture".
- **Weather** (3): Boiling Rain ("O godamn"), Calm Winds, Weeping Sky.
- **Legends** (6): Baldomero the Calvo, Dragon McPusey, Sir Augusitus The XVIII, Vittoria Emiliana, Vittoria
  Frauter, Wynnievere. The vault's Age 0 legend is **Vaelia** (Library entry imported); her council class, rarity and
  bonuses are not in the vault. Augusitus is an Age IV character.
- **Civics** (4): Arcane Scholars, Guild Masters, Innovation Council, Military Academy. The vault's Age 0 civics are
  Moonlit Vigil, Bridal Conquest, Duels of Severance, Leylines Nomads, Sky Glass Burials and Chaos Reverence
  Sacrifices (Library entries imported from Civic.md); their effects are not in the vault.
- **Council**: the Head of State's line ("The supreme leader of the civilization..."), the seat positions themselves
  (High Arbiter, Oracle...), which classes each accepts, and their bonuses (the old seats' own). The Grand Archivist
  (Great Chronicler, Architect or Sovereign; +1 Aureus, +1 Regalia; its description line and its borrowed
  Knowledge Sanctums icon) was added on the user's request (Sept 26, 2026) with no vault source.
- **Tooltip lines in code** (TooltipContent): "Carpal Tunnel Power!", "Upping the uppies 100% upper", "Not starving
  anymore...".
- **Events**: the starter story "The Lost Caravan" (Elder Yarra, Seraphina) is not from the vault. The vault's Age 0
  event is *A Burrowed Name* (Ages 0-VI, on a Ritual Seventh), not yet written as Ink.
- **Age stories** (Sept 26, 2026: `AgeOfDesolation.ink`, `AgeOfRenewal.ink`, `Ballads.ink`): their quotations and
  descriptions are the vault's (The Inescapable Hunger, Great Plague, Memory Field...), with em dashes written as
  hyphens for the game font, but the short connective lines between them (outcome verses, button labels, the choice
  descriptions without a vault source) and the ballad *The Ruin-Song* as a three-verse storyline are placeholders to
  replace with vault text. The Age of Renewal has no epigraph (its note is empty); the Age II assets (Behemoths,
  Glyphs, Embers) have no content yet. Map feature names and lines come from the vault's Age 0 imagery; "Resting
  Field", "Survivor Hamlet" and "Survivor Architecture" are game names for vault passages.
- **World generation** (Sept 27, 2026, `Resources/World/World.asset`, `Tiles/`): the six biomes the owner named
  (Violet Grove, Great Expanse, Taiga, Magical Rift, Wind Plains, Auric Grasslands) have no vault notes, so they carry
  no description; the ground they lay down is named after them as placeholders (Violet Woods, Violet Glade, Expanse
  Steppe, Expanse Scrub, Taiga Forest, Taiga Bog, Rift Scar, Windswept Plain, Wind Mesa, Auric Meadow, Auric Copse),
  and so are the generic grounds (Open Sea, Shallows, Lake, Peaks, Highlands, Foothills, Open Plains, Scrubland,
  Marsh, Woodland). The S1, S3, S4 and S6 catalogs reuse the four Age 0 environments as placeholder biomes (Survivor
  Architecture, Crumbling Infrastructure, The Golden Ash, Broken Leyline Channels, with their vault lines); S7's is
  "Sector 7 (placeholder)". The handmade tiles' one-line comments are design notes, not lore.
- **Civilization layer on the map** (Sept 27, 2026, `World.asset` → grandfields, enclaves, threats, settlements):
  the rules follow Arcanoria.md's Map Features, but these parts have no vault words and are placeholders:
  settlement names ("Developing Town 1", "Outpost 2"); the enclaves' origin wounds (Independent Watch Community,
  Traders Rest; The Moonlit Gardens uses Arcanoria.md's own line); every suzerainty yield, binding yield, cost and
  City Development weight; which binding a Major Settlement's ground suggests (Flux by water, Crystal on high stone,
  Resonance on coherent ground, Luminance where magic gathers, Strand among remnants, Cindergale on dry hot land,
  Void where dissonance lingers); the Vibrational Fallout Scar's line (paraphrases Great Harmonic Loom.md); the Atonalis
  Nest arriving in Age II (the vault shows Atonalis by then; nests are named for later Ages); the Glimmerfern Grove
  and Emberwhisper Vein substrates (their notes say only "moonlit groves" and "Resonates with Aetherlight"); and the
  Capital improvement that raises Government Capacity (no building is named yet, so capacity stays at one).
- **Expeditions** (Sept 27, 2026; World.asset `expeditions`, all proposals): the vault has no Expedition note, so
  legend-led parties in place of scouts and settlers are the owner's direction and game design: the slots (one, two
  per point of Government Capacity, one per Hollow Watchpost), a party of four, the Director's strength by Soul
  Leitmotif (the class affinities of Stellar Legacy Score.md name themes such as Truth or Momentum, not these
  effects), the hardship and mishap numbers, the mishap names and notices, and the settlers' cost (5 citizens, 15
  Elderwood). The "Expeditions" technology card (formerly "Wasteland Scout") keeps its line from the owner's sheet,
  "A poor soul in search of a better future." Vault hooks not used yet: the Spiraling Conscription civic (Civic.md:
  Spellweavers with critically low Composure as state-sanctioned expedition units) and the Legend Opus "An
  Expedition Through the Impossible" and "The Expedition's Last Survivor".
- **Party size** (Sept 27, 2026; World.asset `expeditions.shapes`, all proposals): the owner asked that solo, duo,
  trio and four-plus parties behave differently (alone: faster, less attrition, retreat, go missing in action, but
  no help, more strain; groups: slower, more issues and quarrels, more effective). The numbers are game design:
  pace 1.25/1.1/1/0.85, wear 0.8/0.9/1/1.1, hardship 1.35/0.9/1/1.1 plus solitude 0.75 and company friction 0.25 a
  Seventh, mishap risk 0.8/0.95/1.1/1.25, mishaps handled by the others 0/30/45/55% (35% of the harm lands), ambushes
  slipped 50/20%, retreat pace 1.5/1.25 (trio and company cannot), a lone legend going to ground from Fractured (and
  instead of breaking), missing for 2 Sevenths plus 1.5x the walk home, turning up at attrition 40 and fatigue 60.
  "The Expedition's Last Survivor" (Legend Opus) is the natural story for a legend who turns up alone.
- **Legends' souls** (Sept 27, 2026; Resources/Legends/LegendSettings.asset, all proposals awaiting D08): where each
  Composure state begins (Pristine below 10, Clouded from 10 with a resting baseline of 20, Fractured 40, Spiraling
  70, Surrender 100); what strains it (only seated legends carry the Age Crisis, 0.4 a Seventh unannounced and 0.9
  named, and grieve the share of the people who die, 50 per whole population, at most 15 a Seventh) and what mends it
  (2 a Seventh resting, 0.5 leading a unit, 0.25 seated; 12 for a rank reached); council bonuses at 90% Fractured and
  70% Spiraling; a Motif Awakening needs 7 Sevenths Fractured or deeper and is worth 8 renown; the Awakened State
  doubles council bonuses for 7 Sevenths, then collapses to 55; binding scores start at 5 (Apprentice), +3 per
  Ornament, +2 per evolved trait; drawn origin traits fit a Scaled Cost budget by rarity (Common 0 ... Mythic 150).
  The vault's own numbers (the +5 of a primary binding, each origin trait's Binding Effect, the proficiency table)
  are used as written. The six placeholder legends draw their traits; their Soul Leitmotif is their class's affinity.

## Gaps and contradictions in the vault

For the vault (nothing there was changed).

- **Glimmerfern.md** still says "Resonates with Moonshine." The game now calls it Lunehymn; "Moonshine" is an alias
  of the Lunehymn entry so the line still links.
- **The Inescapable Hunger.md** holds three tellings of the crisis plus pasted chat replies ("If you imagine this
  dropped into your in-game codex...", "Below is an updated, more agronomically grounded version...", "Here are the
  concrete adjustments made and why..."). The import uses the second, fullest telling.
- **Unclosed italics** (closed by the import): Waltz Pillar.md's epigraph, Emberwhisper.md, Glimmerfern.md.
- **Empty notes the Age of Desolation links to**: Old World Remnants, Minor Note, Major Note, Age of Renewal,
  District, Piety, Humanity, Lost Cycle, Unison, Dyad Chord, Triad Chord, Tetrad Chord, Great Spellweaver (image
  only), Great Concertist, Legend Title, Magnum Opus, Lesser Opus, Lyrical Fragment, the seven Fragments, Resonance
  Anchors, Grand Thread Rings, Strand Pool. Where another note defines the term (Minor Note, the chords, Great
  Spellweaver, the Great Spellweaver classes), the entry quotes that passage instead.
- **No note at all** for terms the game uses: Head of State, Faith, Food, Housing, Vagrants, Citizens, Satisfaction,
  Morale, Heartlands, Residential District, the twelve Phases and four Echoes (entries quote Cycle.md's lines),
  Auric Peach (the entry quotes The Inescapable Hunger).
- **Design notes inside lore notes**: Arcanoria.md is the map design (the world's description came from the
  Premise of Gateway To Genesis.md); Cataclysmic Aftermath.md is mostly resets, the Master-Key and endings; Seventh,
  Phase and Echo mix the calendar with game timing ("3 real life minutes", "All the game phases are:").
- **Wording**: Seventh.md "the 21th Seventh"; Age Crisis.md "the even of [[A Brown Auric Peach?]]"; Civic.md
  "a slaver causus belly"; some civic entries lack the `**Name | kind | Ages | tone**` header (Cooking Guilds,
  the two Demi-Human trade entries).
- **Map canon still missing** (for the map's civilization layer): Resonance Anchors.md, Grand Thread Rings.md,
  Sacred Site.md, District.md, Capital.md, Major Settlement.md and Developing Town.md are empty or one line; there
  are no notes for Outpost, Trade Nexus, Trade Routes, Resource Grandfield, City Development, Administrative Authority,
  Government Capacity, Suzerainty, Composure's map use, or the ten enclave families (only Indulgent Enclave.md
  exists, and it is empty). Arcanoria.md holds the rules; the individual notes do not.
- **Territory and beauty** (Sept 27, 2026, game-side proposals): Arcanoria.md says the Capital "anchors all
  Administrative Authority" and Major Settlements are "major anchors" with "secondary zones of control", but not how
  far authority reaches, how it grows, what limits it (Administrative Capacity, strain, drift) or that Trade, Havens,
  grandfields and Outposts pull land too; the game's territorial pull, passive adoption and wide/tall thresholds are
  inventions awaiting canon. **Beauty** as a property of a place (and the beauty of each terrain and site) has no
  vault source at all; nor does terrain "governance difficulty". Achievement.md's "Lose any settlement beyond the
  Administrative Authority of your borders" is the only hint that authority can recede.
- **Council structure**: the vault has seven Great Spellweaver classes and a council of "6 Legends and a Head of
  State" (Achievement.md), but no rule for which positions the six legends hold or whether the Head of State holds
  one of them. The game keeps its six positions, each accepting several classes; none accepts the Great Chronicler yet.
- **Units and the Act I tree** (Sept 27, 2026): "Builder" is a game name with a placeholder line (the Wasteland Scout
  and the Settler were retired the same day for expeditions of legends, above). The owner's Act I technology sheet
  is placeholder text (typos kept as written); effects with no system yet (Resource Shed and its capacity, innovation per citizen, Keynote Relics, Vital
  Winds, Resource Capacity) are named but do nothing; the Aurean Winds weather does not exist, so Celestial
  Astrology's eureka cannot be met; Era Score values per deed are proposals (Ages.md defines Era Score, not its sources).
- **Legend Trait.md** (read by the game since Sept 27, 2026; the import lists each line): 16 personality traits have
  no line of speech (Moody and Selfless write `_"."_`; Imaginative, Irrational, Nihilistic, Dogmatic, Hermetic,
  Paranoid, Mystical, Scattered, Principled, Cynical, Skeptical, Naive, Self-Deceiving, Presumptuous have none).
  Thirteen Cognitive & Logic rows write their middle-trait path as prose ending in a citation residue
  ("Arcanorian-Bible.pdf", left out of the game's text), so those traits cannot evolve yet (Paranoid keeps its one
  tabled path, Cindergale). Selfless's first path names "[[Resources]]", not a binding (Resonance?), so it is not
  read. Empty middle-trait paths: Teasing and
  Apathetic (Luminance), Volatile (Luminance, Flux), Impatient, Patient and Stubborn (all three), Devoted (Strand),
  Impressionable (Strand, Flux), Analytical (Crystal, Resonance), Philosophical (Resonance, Strand), Pragmatic and
  Obsessive (Resonance), Intuitive (Resonance, Luminance). Reverent lists one element, not three. Nineteen middle
  traits named on a path are missing from the middle-trait table (Attention Addict, Auric Proselytizer, Bitter
  Grudge, Clingy Narcissist, Compulsive Liar, Condemned Idealist, Desperate Rumination, Fiery Passion, Hollow Heart,
  Impostor Syndrome, Invisible Color, Loss-Beauty Lunatic, Over-giving Devout, People Pleaser, Risk Lover,
  Suppressed Diamond, Threat Cartographer, Tragic Hypocrite, Witness Memorialist). The binding scale reads
  "1 (Terrible) to 30+ (Master Level)" in its prose but 42+ Master in its table (the game uses the table). The
  Dissonance origin heading lacks its closing `**`, and most Dissonance origins (Heir to Shame... Blind) are names
  without a table row, so none can be drawn. Some effects name systems the game does not have (Weaver-Noble's
  "+200 Gold, +15 Dynasty Prestige", Throne of Puppets Master's "Intrigue capacity"). Still missing for the
  achievements: which middle traits are **maladaptive or toxic** (Bundle of Joy), which middle trait becomes which
  **Apex Trait** (Tathāgata of the Soul Leitmotif; the Apex list has no mapping), and the **Underdog** threshold.
- **Composure.md**: the five states are described, not where they begin or what moves between them; the Clouded
  bullet ends in a stray "[[Formless Masses]]". There is no note for the **Catalytic Abyss of Emotion** (Motif
  Awakening.md and Soul Leitmotif.md define it in passing) or the **Awakened State**, whose "Spell Manifestation" and
  "Collapse and Aftermath" steps are empty.
