# Legends, affection and the ballad journal

Canon source: `C:/Arcanoria Master/Arcanoria/Worldbuilding/Society/Stellar Legacy/Legend Relationship.md`, read September 27, 2026. This implementation does not rewrite the vault.

Relationships save inside each legend's existing progress record. A toward B and B toward A are independent readings. The seven canon stages run from Vowed Nemesis, Forged Rivalry and Frayed Tension through Open Rest to Dim Tuning, Sworn Duet and Kindred Soulmate. Romance is a separate, conditional canon subsystem; these changes implement affection, not attraction or offspring.

Meeting and sharing an event writes a memory. A genuine wound establishes the formative Thread. Each directional reading also has a saved **0–100 affection meter**, starting at **50** (the halfway point within its current stage). Positive and negative experiences shift this meter; it is always clamped to its bounds. **85 or above** arms an upgrade; **strictly below 15** arms a downgrade. Exactly 15 is not a downgrade threshold. Entering a threshold never changes the stage on that same experience: a later matching Relation Growth or Relation Fracture must test the bond. Neutral encounters can move the meter but cannot resolve a stage change. Entering stages I, II and III still costs one, two and three eligible tests; leaving a stage costs its depth. Leaving the relevant threshold clears unfinished tests. Each completed stage change resets the meter to 50, preventing a large delta from skipping stages. Both signs matter, and crossing both stage-II poles records a Deep Mirrored Bond. A thread's original material never changes.

Prototype meter weights: ordinary shared stories/encounters add 3 to established ties; authored positive/negative tests add/subtract 12; direct expedition support adds 18; shared hardship adds 10; unresolved quarrels subtract 18; desertion subtracts 30. Evaded mishaps add no points. Callers can supply an explicit `affectionChange` independently of the test direction. No passive timer moves affection. Stage extrema suppress impossible upgrade/downgrade readiness. The notification feed announces entry into each threshold and separately announces an actual stage change, naming both legends, the direction, and the experience. Remaining in the same threshold does not spam repeat notifications; leaving and returning can notify again. Both readings and their meters appear in relationship details; event co-protagonists also show their meter toward the lead.

Each legend can maintain seven significant outgoing ties. Existing ties continue to develop at capacity; additional acquaintances retain memories without silently evicting anyone. Significant means a formed thread, including a thread that later passes back through Open Rest. Explicit severance releases a slot, preserves history and the other legend's independent reading, and costs five Composure strain. The five-strain cost and permanent closure of the severed reading are prototype choices: the canon's degradation/severance section is unfinished. The UI requires a second click and states the cost. Rekindling, passive decay and shared Opus rewards need authored mechanics and are not implemented here.

Expedition companions record their meeting. Injury and other actual hardship test attachment; a helper and their wounded companion remember support once, even though both the helper and party callbacks run. An unresolved quarrel fractures the two participants' readings. Desertion fractures the remaining legends' feelings toward the deserter without manufacturing reciprocal dislike. Escaped danger creates a memory, not automatic depth. Automatic hardship uses Void, quarrels Cindergale and desertion Strand as formation contexts. Completed stories record their cast; when someone is Fractured or worse, staying through the event tests the cast's bonds (Scholar uses Luminance, Resistor Cindergale, otherwise Resonance). These automatic mappings are prototype interpretations, not authored canon facts about individual legends.

Authored choices can override that default for a directional pair with a tested moment:

```ink
* Stay beside them&C consequences: affection:protagonist > co | Void +1
* Break their trust&C consequences: affection:co > protagonist | Strand -1
```

The source and target accept existing cast roles or full legend names. The binding must be Resonance, Luminance, Flux, Void, Cindergale, Crystal or Strand. The amount is exactly +1 or -1: one meaningful test per directed pair per completed event, never several stages purchased by a large number. This consequence asserts that the authored scene contains a genuine relational wound/test. Reciprocal results need two explicit consequences. Casting changes before completion determine who receives the consequences, as with existing fragment rewards.

Story conditions can read actual named pairs using `affection:Name A | Name B >= 2` (stage -3 to 3), `affection_progress:Name A | Name B >= 85` (meter 0–100), or `relationship:Name A | Name B >= 1` (active significant thread). Unknown/unmet legends fail closed. Roles are not resolved in availability conditions because no event cast exists yet. Severed ties read as stage zero, progress 50, and are not active threads. A save stores experience keys so duplicate callbacks cannot repeat a test; the latest twelve memories are retained for presentation.

The **Ballads & Bonds** button opens two views. Ongoing Ballads shows known ballads, protagonists/co-protagonists, the last verse description, latest consequential choice and date, then the earliest untold verse's live requirements. Completed ballads remain below ongoing ones. Continue rechecks the same volume/story locks, conditions and cooldowns as scheduling; it cannot leap to a later verse or replay a finale. Future narrative prose stays hidden. Legend Relationships shows both directions, Thread, progress toward the next stage, latest shared memory, the seven-slot capacity, and deliberate severance. Portrait tooltips also include relationships. The event cast view identifies roles and a co-protagonist's reading toward the lead; its picker now pages through the entire eligible roster.

Older saves receive empty relationship lists and optional journal fields. Relationships saved before the meter was added retain their stage, thread and memories and start at 50/100. Existing saves with a meter preserve it, including threshold readiness, without replaying notifications at load. No retelling of earlier events is invented. Historical ballads without recaps say so. Saves made after this addition require the updated game, as other schema additions do.

## Sunless research and adaptation

Emily Short describes Sunless Sea's quality-based progression: a core knowledge quality, secondary requirements at some stages, and different ways of reaching discoveries. The practical lesson here is to preserve explicit story state and expose actionable requirements instead of requiring players to remember hidden progress. Source: [The Pull of Nuncio, Failbetter](https://www.failbettergames.com/news/the-pull-of-nuncio).

Sunless Skies connects officer stories across regions and unlocks character deeds through lived circumstances. Our adaptation keeps legends attached to continuing verses and makes their experiences inspectable through the journal. Source: [Sunless Skies: ROLEPLAY, Failbetter](https://www.failbettergames.com/news/sunless-skies-update-roleplay).

Failbetter also describes officers undergoing personal change and the difficulty of fitting relationship content into those arcs. Affection here remains independent of romance; attachment develops through the experiences of the characters involved. Source: [Romance in Sunless Sea, part 3, Failbetter](https://www.failbettergames.com/news/romance-in-sunless-sea-part-3-a-fleeting-clench-and-wriggle-in-the-dark).
