## Baseline review before implementation

- **A shared engine exists.** `BattleResolver.Resolve` and `Begin` use `BattleRun`. Manual card selection is available as a code API, but `WorldSystem.Battles.Fight` immediately auto-resolves every clash. `BattleWindow` displays the forecast/report; `SymphonyWindow` browses a deck. Neither is the playable manual battlefield described in the design. Major/boss/Original Eight encounter restrictions are absent.
- **Army composition is partial.** `OrchestralFormations`, `ArmyRoster`, `CreatureCombat`, and `WorldBattles.PartySide` provide recruitment, templates, leaders, creatures, expedition condition and commanders. Templates currently use front/back/support roles and combat width/reserves. These are not the document's Top/Middle/Bottom spatial lanes or six native positions. Supply, equipment/production tiers, doctrine, full Elite Core and reconnaissance must become explicit score inputs.
- **Two conditions exist; the third is missing before ticket 1.** `CombatSection` carries Integrity and Composure. `BattleLegend.BattleComposure` derives a commander bar from persistent soul strain. Section leader psychology largely follows its section, and Mind Break occurs at zero rather than the elite's Spiraling boundary. Stance Stability and spatial Stance Break consequences do not exist. Ordinary attacks must not cause metaphysical Surrender; extraordinary Parasitic Resonance needs a separate, explicit path.
- **Elemental relationships mostly match.** `HarmonicCircle` correctly uses the primary binding and the pentatonic circle; Ornaments widen casting options. Before ticket 1, resistance is 0.67 rather than **0.5**, and Luminance/Void effectiveness is 1.35 rather than **1.5**. Advantage is already 1.5. Ticket 1 aligns the defaults and adds exact-value regression checks. Custom settings remain explicit overrides.
- **There is no primary combat hex board before ticket 1.** World-map hexes are a different scale. Global river, height, weather, cover, leyline and Dissonance modifiers exist in `Battlefield`, but not per-combat-hex access, physical chokepoints, occupancy, adjacency, melee reach, guarding, Rank Proximity, Skirmishes, retreat, stacking or geometric encirclement.
- **Beats currently mean spending energy.** `BeatsLeft`, `WhyNotPlay`, `Queue`, and `SymphonyTuning.beatsBase` limit how many cards can be queued each round. Both queues resolve, followed by automatic steel/spells, Toll and state checks. The design instead advances simultaneous Countdown Counters with every action, permits every usable card subject to time and state, and supports delayed Wind-Up. Redraw Bell and persistent prepared clocks are absent.
- **Cards/decks are a useful starting point.** `CombatCard`, `SymphonyCards`, `SymphonyDecks`, `LegendGrimoires` and `WorldSystem.Symphony` assemble basic unit cards, kits, personal grimoires, learned arts, war-score cards and commander/creature actions. All five purposes exist. Binding ornaments on a card and automatic ensemble layering are not the tactical stack's exactly one Major Core plus structurally subordinate Minor Notes. Irrevocable commitment, failed prerequisites, stabilization attempts, Reactions and Cascade Chains need implementation.
- **Performance is only an input multiplier.** `Play(..., rendition)` accepts a supplied scalar; `SymphonyTuning.perfect` is 1.25. There is no audio-clock-driven echo minigame, inverse-frequency defensive pattern, 45% Perfect Abjuration rule, Tempo Fever, or accelerated-heartbeat Co-Regulation sequence. A scalar on Guard is not the specified defense mechanic.
- **Psychological agency is missing.** Mind Broken sections lose effectiveness and cannot cast; a broken conductor loses its command gifts. There is no trait-driven Corrupted Instinct hand, same-Measure two-card Corroded Gambit, Cathartic Cadenza, spatial collateral, or character-specific conductor maladaptation.
- **Survival/capture is partial and materially different.** `TryCapture` requires both Mind Break and low Integrity; the design allows capture with substantial remaining Integrity when will/coherence fails. Leaders automatically escape a fallen section through `LeaderLeaves`; zero-Integrity elite Death Knell, escalating Deathblow checks, Piety/Saving Grace, evacuation decisions and Elite Check/Checkmate are absent. Current aftermath splits loss mainly into dead/wounded/captured; recoverable/missing populations need explicit accounting.
- **Relationships and expeditions are partial.** Company attachment boosts, shared battle memories, persistent strain, conditions, attrition and rest exist. Consecutive bonded voices, duet/ensemble synergy, bond-specific emergency protection and witnessed Death Knell shock are missing. Burns/Expose/Blind are numeric marks; the deck does not contain the specified Status Pollution cards, Field/Trauma Alterations, or up-to-two preserved Legend Opus techniques. Prepared 2 Divination + 1 Prophetical retry slots and boss recharge are absent.
- **Forecasts and verdicts are partial.** `SymphonyPower`, `BattlePreview`, seeded multi-run forecasts and mirrored victory/defeat tiers exist. Before ticket 1, `BattleSide.Clone` resets section condition and `BattleSetup.Clone` shares the Battlefield, so forecasts can evaluate healed forces and share mutable inputs. Forecast categories Overwhelming/Favored/Contested/Dangerous/Doomed and explicit objective/Loss Burden are absent. `LegendaryVictory` replaces the casualty category at a provisional <=30% chance; the design requires **Mythical** as an additional distinction on a manual victory from a **Doomed** forecast. Scale-aware elite casualties and commander/equipment losses must change verdict grading.

## Interpretation and fidelity rules

1. Use **16** primary combat hexes: the explicit diagrams and total say 6 friendly + 4 neutral + 6 enemy. The nearby references to three central hexes and a fifteen-hex battlefield are inconsistent prose. Preserve both separate Middle neutral positions.
2. Front/back/support remains a unit role; Top/Middle/Bottom is spatial lane; Rank 1/2 is native front/rear from each army's perspective. Do not conflate these concepts.
3. The documented constants are requirements: elemental 1.5/0.5/1.5, up to +25% Perfect Echo, up to 45% Perfect Abjuration mitigation, at least three Beats for unmodified legato/channeling, two same-Measure corrupted cards for the Gambit, two retained Opus alterations, two Divination plus one Prophetical slot, and the five population-scale boundaries.
4. Values the document does not specify are tunable implementation choices, not new canon: stacking penalty magnitude, Stance bonuses/thresholds, damage and casualty curves, exact doctrine coordinates, BPM/timing windows, Fever thresholds, forecast-category cutoffs, deathblow probabilities and ritual resource costs. Label defaults and get design decisions during their owning tickets; do not silently replace a qualitative rule with an unrelated mechanic.
5. Normal battlefield psychological collapse stops at Spiraling for elites. Corroded Gambit restores temporary tactical regulation, not persistent soul health. Only an explicitly extraordinary effect can approach permanent Surrender.
6. One authoritative state and rules govern both modes. Auto-resolution chooses/performs actions; manual play supplies human decisions and measured performance. The strategic forecast is preserved from before contact. It does not change to improve the player's reward.
7. Failure changes decisions: Stance Break does not delete cards or turns; elite Mind Break changes the hand rather than stunning the player; honest committed enemy intent cannot be rewritten after player input.
